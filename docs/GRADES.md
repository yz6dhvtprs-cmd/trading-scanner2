# How trade setups are graded (share with subscribers)

Every alert carries a grade. Grades come from 10 years of backtested history
plus live paper-trade tracking — never from opinion.

- **A+ (highest conviction)**: wins big on average (+0.50R or more per trade
  after costs), profits ≥ 1.5× losses, trend confirmed on weekly + daily,
  heavy volume behind the move. Tradable with stock or options.
  Live example: 20-day-high breakout with strong trend, trailed stop.
- **A**: solid edge (+0.30R/trade), profits ≥ 1.3× losses. Tradable;
  options via spreads preferred.
- **B+**: modest edge (+0.15R/trade) or a strong pattern on one timeframe
  only. Spreads only, smaller size.
- **B (watch/paper)**: valid pattern, thin or unproven edge. We show it so
  you can learn the read — don't size it.
- **C (paused)**: was losing in live tracking. No alerts until it earns
  its way back. Shorts currently sit here.

House rules: win rate alone never upgrades anything (30% wins on big runners
beats 60% wins on small ones); grades auto-demote when live results slip;
targets come from support/resistance + Fibonacci, stops always defined, and
every text states entry, target, and stop.
